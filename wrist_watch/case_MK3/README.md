# Watch case MK III (screwless, flush strap, sprung crown)

This redesigns the MK II case (`../instructibles_prod/Watch_*_MK_II.stl`, by Sverd Industries). The changes are:

- The back logo is gone.
- The strap now sits flush.
- There are **no screws**. The two M2 ring screws between the lugs are gone.
- There is a real **crown with a spring** instead of a switch that sticks out.
- A printed liner lets the same internals go into a **metal case**.

This is prototype V1, to be printed free on BoilerMAKER FDM before paying for resin or metal. There are two closure options; print both and keep the one that feels better.

The parametric source is `watch_case.py` (build123d + manifold3d); everything here is generated by it. The master copy is in `OneDrive/Agents/3Dprints/watch_case/`.

| Caseback A | `caseback_A_V1` | **Push and twist** (bayonet). It clamps the whole stack against the lip, so no glue anywhere. Has a coin slot. |
| Case B | `case_B_V1` | Closed back, like MK II. |

## How it works
- **Crown.** The stem is an **M2 flat-head screw (DIN 965)** put in from **inside** the case.
  - The head sits in a countersink, which is the outward stop. The head face is also the plunger that presses the switch.
  - The printed cap self-threads onto the screw outside.
  - The cap stops against the case boss after 0.5 mm. That is the hard stop that protects the switch, and you set it by how far you screw the cap on.
  - The carrier's tongue presses the head outward. That gives the return and stops rattle.
- **A, push and twist.**
  - Line up the 3 ticks on the caseback with the 3 ticks on the case bottom.
  - Push in about 0.3 mm against the springs.
  - Turn **clockwise** (looking at the back) 28° and let go. The lugs drop into pockets: that's the click.
  - To open, push in and turn back. The twist zone is entirely below the internal floor, so it never reaches the crown or switch.
- **B, twist-lock bezel.**
  - Line up the 3 dots on the bezel with the ticks on the case flank.
  - Drop the bezel in and turn **clockwise** 28°. It ramps down, then clicks into the pocket.
  - Its lugs run above the PCB; the switch is below it.
- **Strap.** A flat shoulder now fills the space between the lugs.
  - The two ends are deliberately different so you can compare: 12 o'clock is set for a 4.0 mm leather end and 6 o'clock for a 3.5 mm end, each with a 0.3 mm gap.
  - Lug holes are drilled through (Ø1.0), so spring bars can be pushed out from outside.

## Hardware
- 1× M2×5 flat-head screw (DIN 965). Alternatively, file one of the freed M2×6 ring screws to 5 mm, or use the M2×6 with `crown_M2x6` (it sticks out 1 mm more).
- 2× 20 mm spring bars (as now).
- A Ø38 × 1.5 flat mineral crystal (bought). For B, glue it into the bezel. For A, no glue.

## Assembly
**A:**
1. Put the screw into the countersink from inside and thread the cap on until the gap to the boss is about 0.5 mm.
2. Crystal into the case from the back.
3. Dial ring.
4. PCB, LEDs toward the crystal.
5. Carrier: key at 9 o'clock, tongue behind the screw head.
6. Twist on the caseback.

**B:**
1. Screw and cap as above.
2. Carrier: key at 9 o'clock.
3. PCB.
4. Bezel, with the crystal glued in.

## Printing (files are already oriented, no supports)
- Case A: back down.
- Caseback: inner face down.
- Case B: back down.
- Bezel: top down.
- Carriers and dial ring: bottom down.
- Crowns: end face down.

The lug bottoms are flat only for FDM (`LUG_DROP = 0`). For resin or metal, set `PROCESS = "sla" / "cnc" / "slm"` and the lugs curve down 1.6 mm like MK II. That setting also changes the fits. Drill the lug holes 1.0 mm after printing.

## Measure these, then regenerate
- **The switch.** How far its actuator sticks out past the PCB edge, and how far below the PCB it sits. The design assumes the body is flush with the PCB edge and the actuator tip is at r ≈ 19.0, 2.55 mm above the floor (`SW_*`, `Z_CROWN`). If your switch sticks out further, trim its plunger or resolder a lower-profile one.
- **The strap end thickness.** Pick the end that fits best and set both ends to it (`STRAP_T`).
- **The tallest part on the PCB's LED side.** If it is under about 1.5 mm, `GAP_ABOVE_PCB` can drop from 2.5 to 2.0 and both cases get 0.5 mm thinner. Right now A is 12.5 mm and B is 12.2 mm, against 11.6 mm for the MK II body.

## After the prototype
Pick A or B, tune the fits, then make the premium case. The carrier and dial ring stay printed (PETG) in every version; for a metal case they are the insulating liner.

**On campus:**
- BIDC Form 3B+: Rigid 10K / High Temp resin, by reservation.
- Purdue 3D Printing Club: resin at about $0.30/mL.
- BIDC Fuse 1: SLS nylon.

**Online:**
- JLC3DP: black resin, or SLM 316L. 316L needs walls of at least 1.5 mm, so `GROOVE_R` has to change.
- PCBWay: CNC aluminium with bead blast + anodize.
